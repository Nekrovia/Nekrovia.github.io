Server Rules

Rules of the Nekrovia DayZ server — PVP and PVE. Read them before you start playing. Not knowing the rules does not exempt you from them.

☠️ Most important: PVP • PVE • NO RAID — fighting other players is allowed, but bases are a protected zone. Don't raid other people's bases. Don't destroy other people's constructions. Don't exploit bugs. Survive. Build. Fight.
1. General rules 2. Bases 3. PVP 4. PVE 5. Cheats and exploits 6. Behavior 7. Administration 8. Vehicles

⚔️ 01 — General rules

  • PVP is allowed — players may fight each other.
  • Stealing items outside of bases is allowed.
  • The server combines PVP and PVE gameplay.
  • Each player is responsible for the safety of their own gear.
  • Administration is not responsible for items lost during gameplay.

🏠 02 — Bases

  • Base raiding is forbidden.
  • Destroying constructions belonging to other players is forbidden.
  • Opening, destroying, or bypassing the security of other players' bases is forbidden.
  • Exploiting bugs and glitches to get into another player's base is forbidden.
  • Stealing items found inside secured bases is forbidden.
  • Every player has the right to safely store items in their own base.

🔫 03 — PVP

  • Ambushes, player hunting, and fights between players are allowed.
  • Killing other players is part of the gameplay.
  • Exploiting bugs to gain an unfair advantage in combat is forbidden.

🧟 04 — PVE

  • The infected and other threats in the game world can pose a real danger.
  • Exploration and gathering loot are part of the gameplay.

🚫 05 — Cheats and exploits

  • Any kind of cheats or hacks are forbidden.
  • Using ESP, aimbot, wallhack, speedhack, etc. is forbidden.
  • Exploiting game bugs for your own benefit is forbidden.
  • Item duplication is forbidden.
  • Any exploit you find should be reported to the administration.

🤝 06 — Behavior

  • Respect other players.
  • Doxxing and publishing private information is forbidden.
  • Impersonating administration is forbidden.
  • Conflicts between players should stay within the bounds of the gameplay.

⚠️ 07 — Administration

  • Administration may respond to situations not directly covered by these rules.
  • Exploiting loopholes in the rules to gain an unfair advantage may be punished.
  • Administration may issue penalties depending on the offense.

🚗 08 — Vehicle rules

  • Vehicles left outside a secured shelter can be stolen by other players, during both PVP and PVE.
  • A vehicle left unprotected, even near your own base, is treated as unprotected and can be stolen along with everything inside it.
  • If a player wants to protect their vehicle and its contents, they must build it a proper, secured shelter.
  • The mere fact that a vehicle is parked near a base does not mean it is protected. It must be inside a purpose-built, secured vehicle storage space.
  • Vehicles found out in the world can be taken by other players if they are not inside a secured shelter.
  • Stealing a vehicle left outside a shelter includes its contents as well.
  • A vehicle inside a properly secured vehicle shelter cannot be stolen.
  • Obtaining vehicles from a secured shelter through exploits, bugs, glitching, or other game errors is forbidden.
⚠️ Remember: want to protect your vehicle — build it a shelter. A vehicle left to fend for itself can be taken by another player.
