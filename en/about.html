PVPe — combat and survival in one

Nekrovia combines intense PVP fights with a survival-focused PVE atmosphere. It's a server for players who want to compete with others as well as face the threats of Chernarus itself.

⚔️

Unrestricted PVP

Ambushes, player hunting, and open fights are a full part of the gameplay — fighting other survivors is everyday life on Nekrovia.

🧟

Demanding PVE

Infected and other threats of the game world pose a real danger — exploring the map and gathering loot requires care.

🏠

NoRaid

Player bases are a protected zone — raiding, destroying constructions, and exploiting bugs to break in is strictly forbidden.

🚗

Vehicles protected by shelters

A vehicle in a secured shelter is safe. Left unprotected — even close to your base — it can be taken by other players.

👥

LBmaster

Create groups with friends and survive together, plus use an in-game map overview to find your way around Chernarus more easily.

🛠️

Custom

Custom configuration, custom loot, and additional mods deliver gameplay that stands apart from standard DayZ.

Security

Zero tolerance for cheaters

Cheats, hacks (ESP, aimbot, wallhack, speedhack), exploiting bugs, and item duplication are completely forbidden. Active administration monitors the server and responds to community reports.
