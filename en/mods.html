Osada

Osada is our custom, complete gameplay mode. The server splits into two rival factions — Alpha and Beta. Each one builds up its own settlement, earns points through events, and spends them at point-based NPC stations.

Once a settlement reaches a high enough build level, its faction unlocks the ability to attack the enemy. Capturing the target ends the Season and declares a winner — the community that built itself up stronger wins.

Black Market

🕴️

A mysterious dealer who doesn't stay in one place — he appears at rotating, unmarked locations on the map and disappears after a while, only to show up somewhere else. You never know in advance where you'll find him today.

He buys rare and illegal items for casino chips in several denominations, with a per-player sell limit protecting the server economy from abuse. The trading menu works in several languages, including Polish and English.
