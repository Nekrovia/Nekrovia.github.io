Osada

Osada is our custom, complete gameplay mode. The server splits into two rival factions — Alpha and Beta. Each one builds up its own settlement, earns points through events, and spends them at point-based NPC stations.

Once a settlement reaches a high enough build level, its faction unlocks the ability to attack the enemy. Capturing the target ends the Season and declares a winner — the community that built itself up stronger wins.

Black Market

🕴️

A mysterious dealer who doesn't stay in one place — he appears at rotating, unmarked locations on the map and disappears after a while, only to show up somewhere else. You never know in advance where you'll find him today.

He buys rare and illegal items for casino chips in several denominations, with a per-player sell limit protecting the server economy from abuse. The trading menu works in several languages, including Polish and English.

Vehicle Physics

🚗

We rebuilt vehicle handling and mechanics from the ground up — separately for each of the 5 models. A car's technical condition now actually affects how it drives and behaves on the road, not just what you see in its health bar.

A worn-out engine loses power, a bad spark plug can strand you in the middle of nowhere, and a loose wheel will fall off if you push it too hard. Off-roaders in the terrain actually pull with every wheel now instead of getting stuck with one hanging in the air. The good news: almost everything can now be repaired, all the way back to factory-new condition.

How to play with the new mechanics — scenario by scenario
1

🪢 Always buckle up with C

A buckled seatbelt heavily reduces crash damage — it can be the difference between a light shock and dying from the exact same hit. Just keep in mind you can't exit the vehicle, not even bail out mid-flight, while buckled — unbuckle with the same key first.

2

🔄 Engine died mid-drive? Don't get out yet

A worn spark plug can randomly stall the engine while you're driving — the worse the plug, the more often. If you're still in gear when it happens, the car will try to catch on its own right after stalling. If you're stopped or in neutral, shift into a forward gear — that's your shot at a rolling (bump) start. You can try as many times as you like by shifting to neutral and back.

3

🔋 Dead battery? Push the car down a hill

A bump-start works mechanically, not electrically — it completely ignores the battery's charge. Found a car with a dead battery? Push it (or find a slope), get above roughly 10 km/h, and shift into gear — the engine will catch without any charge at all. Note: the battery still has to physically be there and not be fully Ruined — this doesn't replace a missing one entirely.

4

🔩 Tighten your wheels before a long drive

A lug wrench lets you loosen or tighten a wheel's bolts. A wheel left loose will fall off on its own once you go above roughly 20 km/h — it lands as a normal, pickable item next to the car, but you're left driving on three wheels. Check your wheels before a long trip, especially after someone else has been tinkering with your car.

5

🛠️ Repair a damaged part instead of replacing it

Walk up to the damaged part with the right tool in hand and use the repair option: radiator — Epoxy Putty, spark/glow plug — Electronic Repair Kit, engine — Blowtorch (needs a charged gas canister). Each use heals one damage level, so a badly damaged part may take a few uses to get back to factory-new — no more hunting for a replacement across all of Chernarus. The radiator and spark plug still need replacing once fully Ruined — but the engine can be repaired with a blowtorch even from a complete wreck, all the way back to full health.

6

🔥 Close the doors to warm up

A running engine warms up the cabin, but only when every door (and the trunk on some cars) is closed. Driving a soaked, freezing passenger after escaping the water? Shut every door and get the engine running — leave even one open and the heat escapes, no comfort bonus.

7

🏔️ Taking ADA off-road? The drivetrain finally pulls like it should

On ADA (OffroadHatchback), the center and rear differentials are locked while the front stays open — exactly like a real off-roader with a locking rear axle. One wheel hanging off a rock or a stump no longer steals all the engine's power, and the open front still lets you steer smoothly on pavement instead of fighting the wheel.

8

⛽ Planning a long trip? Bring spare fuel

Cars now burn noticeably more fuel than in the base game — crossing half the map on a single tank is no longer a sure thing. Pack a spare jerry can, especially if you're driving a truck or pushing an off-roader hard.

9

🚀 Need a burst of power? Hold Shift

The server may run an admin-set speed limit to keep road driving more pleasant and safer for everyone. Holding Shift (turbo) temporarily bypasses it, giving you full power exactly when you actually need it — like outrunning a chase.
